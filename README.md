### How to Use the Website: 

**The Sign Up/Log In Pages:**
1. Use your name, email, and password to sign up. Don't worry, your passwords are safe with me 😊 (They are hashed)
2. You will be taken back to the Home Page. Log in with the credentials you just used to Sign Up

**The Home Page:**
1. A corporate gobbledygook slogan
2. The published games and the work-in-progress games
3. Pictures of the developMEnt team
4. A link to the feedback/bug report page

**The Game Pages:**
1. A Catalogue of upcoming, unreleased titles
2. A Catalogue of released, purchasable titles as well as links to where they can be purchased
3. A link to the feedback/bug report page

**The Feedback Page:**
1. A place for users to send us feedback on games and suggestions for future games
2. A place to report bugs, steps to produce a bug, and Operating System that the bug was found on
3. A place for the user to ask general questions to the developers
